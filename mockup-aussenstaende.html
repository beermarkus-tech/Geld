Reading this mockup: every card here is just a query result over tagged Konten lines — there's no dedicated claim data of its own. Each card is one claim-type tag (indigo pill); expanding it groups its lines by whichever claim-category tag they also carry (rose labels, e.g. Meal/Team Meal/Hotel) — never summed within a group, so three separately-tagged Meal lines show as three lines, matching your Gsheet's habit exactly. A tag with no category tags at all (the "Dirk Sept" loan, the Amazon return) just shows its lines flat, no grouping layer — same screen, same mechanism, no special-casing. The net total is the only status that exists now: red with the outstanding amount while a claim/loan is open, green ✓ the instant every tagged line plus the settlement transaction nets to zero — no manual "Neu/Eingereicht/Abgerechnet" field anywhere. "Offen"-filtered claims/loans sort first, since that's what you'd actually want to check on any given day. CPAM isn't shown here since it's still on hold — this same screen picks it up automatically once tagging starts there, no separate build needed. On phone, cards stack and expand the same way, tap to open/close.
