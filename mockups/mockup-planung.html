Reading this mockup: Planung is a report, not an editing surface — every category figure here is a live mirror of Verlauf's Plan0/Plan1/Prog; nothing is typed into a category amount on this screen. The global year selector (top-right, both tablet and phone — §1b.2a) picks the planning year; the reference-year column is always selectedYear − 1, always Plan 0 (closed years never change). The 2026 column has its own small source switcher (Plan 0 / Plan 1 / Prog) — a quick lens on how the plan has drifted, not a different planning target; comments and the split stay pinned to Plan 0 regardless of which lens is active. Regulär/Einmal Jahr + the % pill are fully automatic (median-based, §2.8/§3c) — no input control, just the computed result. The 💬 icon marks a per-category-per-year comment (amber when a note exists); click to expand — Steuerausgaben's "Geringere Steuerausgaben" and Urlaube's trip breakdown are shown expanded as an example. The chart plots Budget (green) against stacked Ausgaben (amber) + Rücklagen (purple) for both years, using the confirmed exact formulas from §3c — note 2026 reads distinctly tighter than 2025, which is the point of the visual. On phone, the report becomes a scrolling stack of cards grouped the same way.
