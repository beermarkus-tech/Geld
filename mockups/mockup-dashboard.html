Reading this mockup: Dashboard is now a **pinned alerts band + a grid of individual cards**, each a condensed view that opens a fuller detail view on tap (§1b.2) — Prognose and Status have no separate screens of their own; this page and its cards' detail views are the entirety of both. The alerts band stays pinned above the grid, not itself a card, since these need to be seen immediately (real open Außenstände item, 2026-05 HAM −336,50 €, plus a Rücklagen-check mismatch, each tappable straight through); an all-clear state would collapse this to a single green line rather than disappearing. Prognose is the largest/primary card — Barkonten actual/prognosis (red) against Plan1 (dashed) and Plan0 (dotted), Sparkonten/Geldanlage as background bars, shaped after your real chart's mid-year spike-then-drop pattern. Below it, two new cards that were previously missing a home entirely: Jahresbilanz (the yearly balance summary — Jahresanfang/Einnahmen/Ausgaben/Jahresende, shown here as plain condensed bars; the full version breaks each down by allocation tag with Plan0/Plan1/Prognose overlaid, in the detail view) and Geldanlage-Standort (the fund-location chart — which account(s) actually hold each allocation tag's money; card shows 3 of the real 7 tags, full list in the detail view). Status is condensed to the most-deviating categories only (here: Urlaube, Haustiere, Lebensmittel) rather than the full list — tapping it opens every category. Quickview stays a pure doorway, no chart of its own. Every card's "Details ›" / "öffnen ›" link is where its detail view takes over — exact filter/display controls inside each detail view aren't designed yet, parked as a follow-up. On phone, all cards stack vertically in the same order: alerts → Prognose → Jahresbilanz → Geldanlage-Standort → Status → Quickview.
