Reading this mockup: the selector at top is now a plain, two-step Kategorie → Unterkategorie picker (here: Sonstiges → Urlaube) — no tag search box, since tags are never chosen directly here anymore (§3j). Whatever tags actually exist underneath the selected subcategory get auto-discovered and each becomes its own card, side by side on tablet / stacked on phone; a subcategory with no tags underneath would instead show one plain flat card with no tag pill at all. This year, Urlaube has exactly one — Schottland (teal, matching Verlauf's project-tag color) — so only one card renders, pulling the same real numbers as the Verlauf mockup's "Schottland (automatisch)" row: Budget −6.840 € is the pure Plan 1 sum of its children, untouched by reality; Prognose −6.791 € blends what's now real with what's still mirrored. Inside the card, the content splits at the pivot — Verlauf's last "ok" month (July here) — into two sections. Bereits gebucht (green) lists real Konten transactions, grouped by whichever child tag each one carries (Unterkünfte / Flug & Auto), individually, never summed away — exactly the three July bookings that make up the real −2.705 €, a small, honest 49 € under what was planned for that month. Jan–Jun show no bookings at all, since nothing happened yet for this trip that early. Noch geplant (grey, dashed markers) lists what's still sitting in Plan 1 for August and September, per child, per month — not yet real, and not merged with the booked section in any way. Nothing here is netted or rebalanced automatically; moving a planned amount to a different month, if a booking came in lighter or heavier than planned, stays a manual edit in Verlauf. Allocation tags (Sparen/Anlage/Steuern/Tagesgeld) never appear here at all — out of scope for this screen, treated like sub-accounts (§3j). On phone, the selector stacks as two compact dropdowns above the same card.
